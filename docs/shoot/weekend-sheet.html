Take order

SlotTakeWhat it's for / how
911Audio calibration Checks the lav works and sets the level. Speak at performance volume, then the loudest line. No clipping. Then 10 s of silence for room tone.
921Facial calibration: start Epic Lets the solver learn how the two HMC cameras sit relative to each other. Move the calibration board slowly in front of both cameras: near and far, tilted, covering every part of the frame. Keep the whole board visible in both cameras.
941Neutral face Today's reference face for this performer. Relaxed, eyes open, looking straight ahead, teeth together but not clenched. Hold about 5 s.
931Body calibration Reference pose for the body solve. Stand in the centre with the whole body in frame, arms in A-pose, about 5 s. Follow the Markerless plugin's own steps once it's installed.
001+Performance takes Clap once, in view of the body camera, at the start of every take. Until there's a sync box, that clap is what lines up face, body and audio.
922Facial calibration: end Epic Same as 921, at the end of the session. If the helmet is adjusted significantly mid-session, do another calibration take before carrying on.

Tags: Epic means Epic's documentation requires it (MetaHuman, Calibration Takes). The others are our house rules.
